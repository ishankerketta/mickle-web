Delete Your Account

This page describes how to permanently delete your Mickle account and data, as required by the Google Play Store. Last updated: 24 Aug 2026.

If you have the app installed

  1. Open Mickle
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

This takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email support@diveriontech.com with the subject "Delete my Mickle account," sent from the email address you signed up with. Your account will be deleted within a reasonable time, and you'll receive a confirmation once it's done.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, in one action, at the database level:

  • Your profile and account preferences
  • Every transaction you've recorded
  • Your categories and budgets
  • Any recurring rules and credit card records you've set up

Nothing is retained. There is no separate copy kept for analytics or any other purpose beyond routine infrastructure backups that age out on their own schedule. This is also how you exercise a right to erasure under most privacy laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

Partial deletion

Mickle currently supports full account deletion only — there is no separate option to delete some of your data (for example, just your transaction history) while keeping your account active.
