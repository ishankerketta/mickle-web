Delete Your Data

This page describes how to delete some or all of your logged data in Mickle without deleting your account. Last updated: 24 Aug 2026.

Clear one month

  1. Open Mickle and go to the Months tab
  2. Open the month you want to clear
  3. Tap the trash icon on that month's page and confirm

This removes only that month's transactions. Your account, categories, and budget targets are untouched.

Clear everything

  1. Open Mickle and go to Settings → Clear Data
  2. Choose Clear all transactions and confirm

This removes every transaction you've logged. Your account, categories, and budget targets are kept exactly as they were — this does not delete your account. If you want your account and everything tied to it removed instead, see Delete Your Account.

What's kept vs. removed

  • Removed: the transactions covered by whichever option you choose (one month, or all of them).
  • Kept: your account, sign-in, categories, and budget targets.

Nothing is retained. Cleared transactions are removed at the database level immediately and cannot be recovered. If you want a copy first, use Settings → Export your data before clearing.
